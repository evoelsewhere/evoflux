# Fluxling — EvoFlux pet concept v1

Fluxling is a small morphing synthetic fox-cat companion. Its rounded-diamond
head, paired ribbon-like ears, central sensor, and looping split tail evolve the
geometry of the EvoFlux flow mark into a character rather than attaching the
logo to a generic robot.

## Character signals

- Calm, curious, precise, and trustworthy rather than loud or childish.
- Paired flexible forms represent parallel specialists and coordinated flow.
- A closed, balanced tail loop communicates handoff and verification.
- Graphite and ivory preserve the core brand; indigo and violet appear only as
  restrained state and motion accents.

## Intended states

1. **Idle** — compact, attentive silhouette.
2. **Working** — ribbon forms branch into parallel streams.
3. **Verified** — relaxed eyes and a balanced closed tail loop.
