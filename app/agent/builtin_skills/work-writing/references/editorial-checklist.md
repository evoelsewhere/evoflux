# Editorial checklist

Use the applicable checks before delivering high-stakes or external content.

## Message integrity

- The requested action or conclusion is explicit and placed early.
- Every material fact is supported by the provided source of truth.
- Facts, interpretation, recommendation, and commitment are distinguishable.
- Caveats are proportional and cannot be mistaken for footnotes to a stronger
  claim.
- Terms and acronyms are stable and appropriate to the audience.

## Operational precision

- Names, titles, dates, times, time zones, amounts, units, percentages,
  denominators, links, owners, and deadlines are verified.
- Relative dates are replaced with exact dates when misunderstanding matters.
- Requests identify who acts, what they do, and by when.
- Placeholders are conspicuous and cannot be published accidentally.

## Sensitive communication

- Confidential information is limited to the intended audience.
- Legal, security, personnel, incident, and customer claims do not exceed
  verified facts or granted authority.
- Written content does not include speculation better handled synchronously.
- Tone is direct without minimizing harm, assigning unsupported blame, or
  promising an outcome outside the sender's control.

## Final edit

Remove duplicated context, empty headings, filler transitions, and conclusions
repeated without new value. Read the opening and closing alone: together they
should state the purpose, decision or request, and next action consistently.
