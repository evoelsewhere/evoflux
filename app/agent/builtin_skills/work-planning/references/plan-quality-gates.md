# Plan quality gates

Apply the relevant gates before presenting a substantial plan.

## Outcome gate

- Completion is observable by a user, operator, or decision owner.
- Scope and non-goals prevent adjacent work from silently entering.
- The accountable owner and approval authority are distinct where needed.

## Dependency gate

- Every blocked item names the exact prerequisite and its owner.
- External handoffs have an acceptance condition, not merely a meeting.
- The critical path contains no hidden decision or unowned dependency.
- Parallel work does not assume an unstable shared contract.

## Estimate gate

- Estimates state scope, capacity, uncertainty, and whether they mean effort or
  elapsed time.
- Fixed dates expose the variable: scope, capacity, quality, or risk.
- Discovery-heavy work has a time-boxed proof gate before a delivery promise.

## Rollout gate

- Cohorts, success thresholds, pause conditions, rollback owner, and recovery
  time are explicit.
- Telemetry exists before exposure expands.
- Irreversible actions have authority and proof prerequisites.

## Operating gate

- The plan has a first action, review cadence, decision log, and change owner.
- Risks have early signals and contingencies.
- Completion includes handoff, documentation, cleanup, and operational
  ownership when relevant.
